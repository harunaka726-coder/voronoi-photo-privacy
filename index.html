Privacy Policy

Voronoi Photo · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Voronoi Photo (“we”, “us”, or “the Service”) handles information when you use our website and related features. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

Voronoi Photo is a browser-based creative tool that lets you compose generative photo mosaics using Voronoi diagrams. You can contact us through the contact form linked from the Service.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored on your device

Artwork, uploaded images saved to the in-app Album, language preference, and “first visit” tutorial flags are stored locally in your browser (for example via localStorage or IndexedDB). This data generally does not leave your device unless you export it yourself or clear browser storage.

2.2 Information you send us

If you contact us through a form, we receive the details you choose to submit (such as email address and message content) so we can respond.

2.3 Technical and usage data

Like most websites, we or our hosting providers may automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and approximate timestamps. This helps us operate, secure, and improve the Service.

3. Advertising

The Service may display advertising, including third-party ads (for example Google AdSense or similar networks). Advertising partners may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies to show ads, measure performance, and—where permitted—deliver interest-based advertising.

These partners may collect or receive information about your browser and activity on this and other sites. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies. Where required by law, we will request consent before enabling non-essential advertising cookies.

You can learn more about Google’s advertising practices and opt-out options at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and adssettings.google.com.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use essential cookies or local storage for core features (language, tutorial state, album data). Advertising and analytics partners may set additional cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may limit functionality or personalization.

5. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To respond to support or contact requests
  • To measure usage and diagnose technical issues
  • To display ads and understand ad performance
  • To protect against abuse and comply with legal obligations

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host the site, serve ads, or process contact forms, and when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. Data retention

Local album and preference data remain on your device until you delete them in the Album tools or clear browser storage. Contact messages and server logs are retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

If you access the Service from outside the country where our hosting or providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries that may have different data-protection rules.

10. Your choices

  • Export or delete Album data from the Album page
  • Clear site data in your browser
  • Adjust cookie / ad settings where available
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests

11. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the revised Policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact form linked from Voronoi Photo (Japanese and English forms are provided based on language settings).
